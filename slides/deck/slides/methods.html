5

Within one block of 64 subcarriers we compare interpolation, LMMSE with statistics from the training data, and a small MLP in four variants. The residual variant adds a learned correction to the LMMSE estimate; the blind variant gets no SNR input. As an extension we stack all 32 antennas and reconstruct jointly, a 512-dimensional problem. There we compare joint LMMSE, a Gaussian-mixture estimator from the literature, which is a weighted mixture of local LMMSE estimators, and a larger MLP. (About 60 seconds.)
