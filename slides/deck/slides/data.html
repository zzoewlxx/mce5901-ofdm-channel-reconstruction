We use the public DICHASUS dataset 005x from the University of Stuttgart: real channel measurements in a room where a metal barrier blocks the line of sight, with 32 receive antennas and 1024 subcarriers. The measured CSI is our ground truth; we add controlled noise to the pilots so that we can sweep the pilot SNR. Three runs are for training, one for validation, and the last run is the test set, which we evaluated exactly once at the very end. (About 50 seconds.)
